## Confirm the interpretation

Explain that a registration may require one chair or an Emperor pair. The initial preference objective is separate from compulsory rules. Absence repair first minimizes moved registration units, then distance, then preferences, while keeping front-fill and east-before-west packing compulsory.

Record whether the respondent agrees with each rule and whether this is their opinion or an authorized organizational policy. User confirmation of the dissertation's scope does not replace organizational evidence.

## Category-zone judgments

Show the layout and identify right/left and inner/outer zones consistently with the application's coordinates. For each category, collect:

For each pair of zones, ask which is preferred and how large the difference feels: no difference, small, moderate or large. Record the respondent's own explanation. These verbal categories guide discussion; do not automatically map them to equally spaced numbers. Formal MACBETH requires its full elicitation/consistency method.

Then ask whether a worst-to-best zone improvement matters equally across participant categories. Keep this separate from within-category rankings. Resolve ties or uncertainty explicitly instead of forcing a strict order.

Revisit a few logically overlapping questions to identify misunderstanding or inconsistent judgments. If preferences cannot be represented by an additive model, record this instead of forcing coefficients to fit.

## Separate calibration from evaluation

Calibration examples are used to choose or revise parameters. Evaluation examples must come from other complete synthetic event instances and must not be reused for tuning. Randomize A/B presentation. Record ties, missing judgments and disagreements between respondents. Report agreement only with its denominator and an explicit rule for ties.

Do not create evaluation labels using the solver's existing objective. Human review time and requested manual edits may provide additional operational measures. The current sensitivity examples are illustrations, not a held-out validation set.
